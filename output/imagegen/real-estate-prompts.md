# Real estate portfolio imagery

Mode: built-in `image_gen` tool, two separate generation calls. The outputs were inspected and saved as optimized WebP assets in the project. The original generated PNGs remain in the tool's generated-images directory. These are illustrative architectural scenes, not representations of named client developments or campaign evidence.

## Waterfront residences

Saved asset: `public/images/case-studies/real-estate/waterfront-residences.webp` (1672 × 941).

Final prompt:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: wide architectural hero for a premium UAE real estate PR sector portfolio webpage.
Primary request: Create a high quality, photorealistic architectural editorial photograph of an imagined contemporary luxury waterfront development in the UAE. This is atmospheric illustrative imagery, not a depiction of any named client building.
Scene/backdrop: tranquil waterfront at early golden hour, pale warm sky, subtle distant skyline without recognisable landmarks.
Subject: refined sculptural residential towers in warm limestone, deep bronze architectural fins, generous terraces and glass, composed with a quiet foreground reflecting pool and restrained landscaping.
Composition/framing: landscape 16:9, professional architectural photography, strong geometric lines, architectural volume on right two thirds and spacious calmer sky on left suitable for a title overlay. Correct verticals, believable scale and material detail.
Lighting/mood: warm natural side light, soft atmospheric distance, luxurious and contemplative, understated editorial quality.
Color palette: ivory, sand, muted bronze, smoky blue water.
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermarks, no people, no signage, no press clippings. Do not imitate an identifiable existing development. A single coherent photograph, no collage.
```

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: supporting editorial image for a premium UAE real estate communications portfolio website.
Primary request: A high quality photorealistic architectural editorial photograph of an imagined luxury residential wellness atrium. Atmospheric illustration only, not a named existing property.
Scene/backdrop: tranquil double-height indoor courtyard in a contemporary UAE residence, pale travertine colonnade, a sculptural curved staircase, restrained indoor greenery, a still shallow reflecting pool.

Lighting/mood: soft daylight falling through a high skylight, warm stone with gentle shadows, quiet and spacious, refined luxury magazine quality.
Color palette: warm ivory, sand, muted olive, bronze details.
Materials/textures: visibly natural stone grain, brushed bronze, subtle water reflections, lifelike scale.
Constraints: no people, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no signage, no press coverage, no identifiable existing building. Single coherent photograph, no collage.
```
